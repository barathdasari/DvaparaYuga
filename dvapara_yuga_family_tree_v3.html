☸ Lineage Cosmos — Guide

An interactive visualisation of the Dvapara Yuga dynasty — from primordial deities through the Mahabharata war to the dawn of Kali Yuga.

Navigation

  • Click any node to open the character profile.
  • Drag nodes to arrange — they pin in place (red ring).
  • Right-click a pinned node to release it to physics.
  • Double-click to collapse/expand all descendants.
  • Scroll or pinch to zoom; drag background to pan.

Relationship Lines

  • Amber dashed — Standard marriage
  • Violet dashed — Niyoga (sanctioned levirate union)
  • Blue solid — Divine boon (god summoned)
  • Emerald dashed — Born of Yajna (fire sacrifice)

Node Sizes & Rings

  • Larger circles = major epic characters. Smaller = peripheral.
  • Green dashed ring = Chiranjeevi (immortal)
  • Red ring = Pinned node
  • Diamond shapes = union nodes (hover for type)
